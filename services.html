What Meter-Sathi offers to make your commute fair and transparent

Meter-Sathi helps commuters verify fares, track rides, and understand official tariffs for auto rickshaws and taxis. Below are the core features we provide — each designed to make your journey fairer and more transparent.

Fare Auditing

Compare the meter fare with the official tariff instantly. We calculate base fare, distance charges, and night surcharges so you can spot overcharging quickly.

Ride Tracking

Start and end ride tracking using your device's location (permission-based). See distance covered and get a live official fare estimate during your trip.

Tariff Reference

Access up-to-date tariff rules for different vehicle types and cities. We show base fares, per-kilometer rates, and night-time multipliers for clarity.

Commuter Support

Guides and tips on how to handle fare disputes, check driver credentials, and report repeated overcharging incidents to local authorities.
