Frequently Asked Questions

What is Meter-Sathi?

Meter-Sathi is a web platform designed to help Mumbai commuters, newcomers, and students verify auto and taxi fares in real time. It aims to protect passengers from unfair practices like fast meters and random fare demands.

How does the fare calculation work?

When you click "Start Ride", our system uses your device's GPS to track the route and calculate the exact distance. It then automatically applies the official Maharashtra Transport Department rules, including accurate night charges, to calculate the correct fare.

How do I know if I am being overcharged?

At the end of your trip, simply enter the meter reading into the site. Meter-Sathi will compare the official GPS-calculated fare with the meter reading you entered and instantly notify you if it is a "Fair Fare" or if you are being "Overcharged".

Do I need to download or install an app?

No installation is required! Meter-Sathi is a web application, meaning it works seamlessly on any browser on your smartphone. Just open the site and ride.

What features are planned for the future?

We are continuously working to improve the platform. Our future scope includes downloadable trip reports, multilingual support, a toggle for taxi/auto modes, integration with official complaint portals, and data analytics to highlight overcharging hotspots in the city.
