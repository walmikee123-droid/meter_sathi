About Meter-Sathi

Empowering commuters with transparent, real-time fare tracking.

Navigating bustling streets in and around Mumbai shouldn't come with the stress of fare disputes. Meter-Sathi was born out of a simple idea: every commuter deserves to pay exactly what is fair, without second-guessing the meter.

We bridge the gap between official regional transport tariffs and daily travel. By utilizing real-time GPS tracking and up-to-date government fare charts, our platform instantly calculates your journey's true cost—accounting for base fares, per-kilometer rates, and even night surcharges.
