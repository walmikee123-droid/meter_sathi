Our Core Services

Comprehensive, high-tech solutions for a stress-free and completely transparent commute.

Live GPS Telemetry

We utilize military-grade smartphone telemetry to map your exact trajectory. Our advanced algorithm actively filters out "GPS drift" when you are stopped at traffic lights, ensuring you only pay for true physical movement.

  • High-frequency 1Hz polling
  • Automatic drift cancellation
  • Sub-5-meter accuracy targeting

Algorithmic Fare Processing

No more mental math. Meter-Sathi calculates everything dynamically. If your ride crosses the midnight threshold, the app automatically splits the distance, applying standard rates before midnight and 25% surcharges strictly afterward.

  • Complex day/night segmentation
  • Base-fare distance absorption
  • Real-time tariff projection

Multi-Vehicle Support

Whether you hail a classic Auto Rickshaw, a standard Kaali-Peeli Taxi, or an AC Cool Cab, we have the exact regional transport authority rates hardcoded into our engine. Switch seamlessly between vehicle modes with a single click.

  • Auto Rickshaw (₹26 base)
  • Non-AC Taxi (₹31 base)
  • AC Taxi support

Dispute Arbitration Toolkit

If the physical meter significantly deviates from our verified calculations, Meter-Sathi provides a "Meter Difference" breakdown. Use this hard data to challenge overcharging, or utilize our external complaint portal for official grievances.

  • Instant difference highlighting
  • Verifiable trip logs
  • Fraud deterrence

Trip History Logging

Every ride you take is automatically logged in your local history. You can review past routes, check the total distance covered, and compare the calculated fare over time to build a solid travel record.

  • Encrypted local storage
  • Date and time stamping
  • Exportable route logs
