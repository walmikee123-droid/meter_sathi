Everything you need to know about Meter-Sathi

Meter-Sathi is a specialized web platform designed to help Mumbai commuters, students, and tourists verify auto-rickshaw and taxi fares in real-time. We help protect you from overcharging and fast meters.

When you start a ride, our system uses GPS tracking to measure the distance covered. It then applies official government tariffs, including base fares and night-time surcharges (12 AM - 5 AM), to calculate the exact legal fare.

Simply compare the meter reading shown by the driver with the fare displayed on Meter-Sathi. If our GPS-calculated fare is significantly lower than the physical meter, it indicates a fast or tampered meter.

No download is required! Meter-Sathi is a lightweight web application that runs directly in your browser. You can "Add to Home Screen" for an app-like experience without using storage space.

You can use our official fare calculation as evidence to talk to the driver. If the driver refuses to cooperate, you can use our "Complaint" feature to record the incident for reporting to the local RTO authorities.
