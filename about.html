The Story Behind Meter-Sathi

Building trust between commuters and drivers through technology.

Every day, millions of people rely on Auto Rickshaws and Taxis to commute across the city. Unfortunately, fare disputes, inaccurate meters, and confusion over night surcharges have become a daily frustration for many. Meter-Sathi was born out of a simple necessity: the need for absolute transparency and fairness in public transportation.

By tapping into the high-precision GPS hardware already built into your smartphone, Meter-Sathi acts as a highly accurate, incorruptible digital meter. We cross-reference your movement against exact municipal tariff data to guarantee that the price you see is the exact price you should pay.

Our Mission

To empower everyday commuters with real-time, tamper-proof fare transparency, while providing a seamless framework to resolve disputes and promote fair practices among drivers.

Our Vision

To become the absolute standard for ride verification across all major Indian cities, eliminating fare anxiety completely and fostering a culture of honest transportation.

The Core Pillars of Meter-Sathi

Precision GPS Engineering

Our algorithms filter out GPS drift and spikes. We sample your location every second and use advanced smoothing to calculate true distance, totally immune to physical meter tampering.

Dynamic Municipal Tariffs

Whether you enter a ride at 11:55 PM or 4:00 AM, our system automatically segments your distance to accurately apply standard rates and 25% night surcharges flawlessly.

Commuter Protection

We don't just calculate fares; we arm you with the data. If you are overcharged, our integrated complaint routing system lets you file a report backed by hard, irrefutable GPS logs.
