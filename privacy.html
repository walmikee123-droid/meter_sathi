Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy: Your location data is your business. We believe in absolute transparency regarding how we handle your data. Meter-Sathi only collects what is absolutely necessary to compute your fare and resolve disputes. We do not sell your data to advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Meter-Sathi, we collect specific types of information to ensure the app functions correctly:

  • Location Data: When you press "Start Ride", we collect high-frequency GPS coordinates (Latitude, Longitude, Timestamp, and Accuracy). This tracking completely stops the moment you press "End Ride", "Pause", or close the app.
  • Account Information: If you create an account, we store your name and authentication credentials securely via Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Complaint Media: Any photos, text, or route logs you explicitly attach and submit via our Complaint portal.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Fare Calculation: Location data is processed locally on your device in real-time to compute distance and apply the correct algorithmic tariff.
  • Dispute Arbitration: If you file a complaint, your submitted route log and media are securely transferred to our database to build your case file.
  • App Improvement: We may analyze anonymized usage telemetry (e.g., app crashes, feature usage) to improve the stability of Meter-Sathi.

3. Data Storage and Security

We leverage enterprise-grade security protocols to protect your information. All account and complaint data is encrypted in transit and at rest using Google Firebase infrastructure.

We do not maintain a permanent historical log of your movement on our servers. Unless you submit a complaint, your route data is cleared from device memory when you press "Reset".

4. Third-Party Sharing

We will not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. We may only disclose data if required by law enforcement or municipal transport authorities in the course of investigating an official complaint submitted by you.

5. Your Rights and Controls

You have full control over your data. You may request a complete export of your account data or request the deletion of your account and associated complaint records at any time by contacting our support team at privacy@metersathi.com.
