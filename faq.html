Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about your journey with Meter-Sathi

Meter-Sathi is a web platform designed to help Mumbai commuters, newcomers, and students verify auto and taxi fares in real time. It aims to protect passengers from unfair practices like fast meters and random fare demands.
When you click "Start Ride", our system uses your device's GPS to track the route and calculate the exact distance. It then applies official tariff rules, including day/night rules, to estimate the correct fare for your journey.
At the end of your trip, enter the meter reading into the site. Meter-Sathi compares the GPS-calculated official fare with the meter reading you entered and notifies you if the meter appears fair or if you are being overcharged.
No installation is required. Meter-Sathi is a web application that runs in your mobile browser — open the site and start tracking your ride immediately.
Planned features include downloadable trip reports, multilingual support, a toggle for taxi/auto modes, integration with complaint portals, and analytics to highlight overcharging hotspots.
