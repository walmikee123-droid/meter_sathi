Knowledge Base

Find answers to the most commonly asked questions about Meter-Sathi's tracking, billing, and complaint systems.

Meter-Sathi uses your phone's internal high-accuracy GPS to actively track your latitudinal and longitudinal coordinates every second. We then map this raw data to a mathematical model. We absorb the first 1.50km into the Base Fare. Anything beyond that is multiplied by the Per Km Rate. If your ride crosses the midnight threshold, the algorithm splits the exact distance down to the meter, ensuring the 25% Night Surcharge is only applied to the distance covered after midnight, not the entire ride.
Meter-Sathi calculates the fare based on the distance actually traveled by the vehicle, just like the physical meter attached to the dashboard. If the driver takes a longer route, both Meter-Sathi and the physical meter will reflect a higher fare. We strongly recommend running a navigation app (like Google Maps) simultaneously to ensure the driver is taking the shortest possible path.
Standard smartphone GPS modules naturally "drift" when stationary, bouncing a few meters back and forth, which can falsely add up to extra distance. To protect you from overpaying, Meter-Sathi uses an advanced anti-drift algorithm. When your speed drops below 1 km/h for more than 4 seconds, we flag the vehicle as Stationary and temporarily pause distance accumulation. The moment you start moving again, tracking instantly resumes.
Currently, Meter-Sathi focuses purely on distance-based tariff calculation. We do not add complex waiting-time charges into the expected tariff. Because of this, you might notice the physical meter is slightly higher (a few rupees) than our calculation if you were stuck in dead-stop traffic for a prolonged period.
