Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your data.

At Meter-Sathi, your privacy is our priority. We believe in complete transparency about how your data is handled while you use our platform to check fares and track rides.

1. Information We Collect

To provide accurate fare estimates, we request access to your device's GPS/Location services. We also collect basic, non-personally identifiable usage data (like device type or browser) to ensure the app runs smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your location data is used strictly in real-time to calculate the distance between your starting point and destination. This allows us to apply the correct local tariffs and night surcharges for your journey.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal data. We may use third-party APIs (such as mapping or routing services) to calculate distances. These services only receive the geographic coordinates necessary to draw the route.

4. Data Security

We implement standard security measures to protect against unauthorized access. Location calculations are primarily handled dynamically, meaning we do not store your permanent travel history on our public servers.

5. Your Choices

You have full control over your data. You can deny or revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings, though this will limit the app's ability to automatically calculate live fares.
