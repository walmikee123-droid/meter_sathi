About Meter-Sathi

Meter-Sathi is a smart fare-checking platform designed to help users verify auto and taxi meter fares quickly and accurately.

Our goal is to promote fair pricing, transparency, and convenience for daily commuters. With Meter-Sathi, you can check estimated fare before or after your ride and avoid overcharging.
